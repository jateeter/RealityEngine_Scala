## Why this is a pointer

`ARBITER_ARCHITECTURE.md` describes how a machine folds its collection of
potential outputs into the one Reality Event it presents per instant — the
`arbiterRule` axis, and how it differs from the `outputMergeTransformation`
fold and from the cell arbitration registry. Three separate things are called
"arbitration" in this system, and the document that tells them apart has to say
the same thing to every runtime or the disambiguation is worthless.

It was a master with no pointers: one authoritative copy in `RealityEngine_CI`
and nothing in the engine repositories naming it. That is the failure mode this
scheme exists to prevent, one step earlier than drift — not three copies
disagreeing, but three repositories implementing a contract with no local sign
that the contract is written down elsewhere. The natural repair for "I need to
describe the arbiter here" is to write a local description, and then there are
two masters.

Governance has one home. `RealityEngine_CI` holds `SURFACE_SPEC.md`,
`MACHINE_CONCEPT.md`, `DEPLOYMENT_CONTRACT.md`, the four `docs/*_CONTRACT.md`
files, and this; `scripts/check-surface-specs.sh` enforces that each has exactly
one master and that every pointer is still a pointer.

This repository owns its implementation of the arbiter, not the definition of it.
